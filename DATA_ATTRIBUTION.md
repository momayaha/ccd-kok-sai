# Data attribution and rights

## Ground measurements
Thailand Pollution Control Department (PCD), Environment and Pollution Control Office 1 (Chiang Mai), performed all measurements. Cumulative Round 22 report: https://epo01.pcd.go.th/th/information/list/2682 . Reference station coordinates: September 22–26, 2025 campaign (https://epo01.pcd.go.th/th/information/list/2543). The station coordinates are references rather than universally verified sampling positions. Cite both the reports and the machine-readable compilation by Jetnipit Sinwisitsophon (2026), v1.0: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23330747 . The supplied original README declares CC BY 4.0 for the compilation (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The DOI metadata is corroborated by NLM Dataset Catalog but the Zenodo record could not be opened during preparation; verify the actual terms and underlying PCD redistribution permission before public release. This package includes only the relevant compilation CSV subset and derived tables, not original report PDFs or the third-party dashboard. No censoring substitution was used for classification.

## Satellite observations
Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2025–2026. Sentinel-2 MSI harmonised Level-2A: https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/COPERNICUS_S2_SR_HARMONIZED . Data accessed and processed using Google Earth Engine. Copernicus Sentinel data licence: https://ewds.climate.copernicus.eu/licences/ec-sentinel . Full images are not included.

## Map
River data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Generalised country boundaries: WDBII/GMT via Basemap; inset coastlines: GSHHG via Basemap. Attribution in Figure 1 is retained. No OSM geometry cache is included in this release candidate. If adding the cache, preserve ODbL attribution and applicable database obligations.

## Project-authored material
Proposed: MIT for author-controlled Python/JavaScript code; CC BY 4.0 for author-controlled documentation and original analysis outputs, subject to third-party terms and institutional rights. These choices are for author approval, not an assertion that rights over third-party data can be relicensed. This release candidate has no blanket licence. Do not publish until the author confirms code/data rights and completes the release checklist.
