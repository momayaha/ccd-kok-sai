# Data attribution and rights

## Ground measurements
Thailand Pollution Control Department (PCD), Environment and Pollution Control Office 1 (Chiang Mai), performed all measurements. Cumulative Round 22 report: https://epo01.pcd.go.th/th/information/list/2682 . Reference station coordinates: September 22–26, 2025 campaign (https://epo01.pcd.go.th/th/information/list/2543). The station coordinates are references rather than universally verified sampling positions. Cite both the reports and the machine-readable compilation by Jetnipit Sinwisitsophon (2026), v1.0: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23330747 . The supplied original README declares CC BY 4.0 for the compilation (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The author subsequently confirmed that the Zenodo source record displays CC BY 4.0. PCD website terms (https://pcdweb.pcd.go.th/terms, consulted 19 November 2026) permit redistribution for education and academic research for non-commercial purposes with attribution. This package is distributed for such purposes. The CC BY 4.0 licence of the compilation does not waive any applicable upstream rights or conditions. No permission for commercial use of underlying PCD material is asserted. Consult PCD for uses beyond its published terms. This package includes only the relevant compilation CSV subset and derived tables, not original report PDFs or the third-party dashboard. No censoring substitution was used for classification.

## Satellite observations
Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2025–2026. Sentinel-2 MSI harmonised Level-2A: https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/COPERNICUS_S2_SR_HARMONIZED . Data accessed and processed using Google Earth Engine. Copernicus Sentinel data licence: https://ewds.climate.copernicus.eu/licences/ec-sentinel . Full images are not included.
